By contrast `O0x13e04=0xf8114422` gives the privileged identity section at `0xf8100000`, domain1, AP1, XN0, NS0. The preserved [M8M base definition](https://github.com/hypoxic/stubby-1.60.1/blob/d670fe97469f4fbac35fcf67fbab81c7ea84f153/configs/config-sc2000.h#L146-L149) also names `PERIPHBASE=f8100000`; its other layout names are not transplanted. This agreement makes `f8100000` a better explicit virtual-board CBAR candidate, not a unique Nikon silicon identification.

The canonical model therefore sets the upstream Cortex-A7 `reset-cbar` property and maps the existing upstream A15MPCore block at `0xf8100000`. The original CBAR consumer produces GICD `base+0x1000` and GICC `base+0x2000`; the host does not fill descriptor pointers. GICv2 IRQ/FIQ, SPI250 and generic timer PPI29 keep their upstream implementations. No guest page table, CPU instruction, exception PC or handler is patched. The earlier `1d000000` receipts retain their historical hypotheses. An aborted frame's RAM requirement is distinct from this underlying translation error; expanding its stack alone is not a fix.
